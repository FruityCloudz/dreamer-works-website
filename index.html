Dreamer Insurance™

PROTECTION & PEACE OF MIND

Accidents happen, but your investment deserves protection! Dreamer Insurance offers coverage for eligible animatronics against fire, storm damage, and accidental destruction.

Our specialized Companion Protection plans help owners safeguard their valuable machines and keep their attractions ready for the next generation of visitors.

Certain plans also include intellectual property licensing and copyright administration for covered animatronic characters. Please consult your policy agreement for the complete terms of ownership, licensing, and commercial use.

"Protect your companion's imagination!"

Dreamer Company™

INTELLIGENCE FOR TOMORROW

Meet the future of animatronic technology! Dreamer Company develops specialized AI chips designed to improve movement, personality recognition, and interactive performance.

Eligible Dreamer Insurance customers may receive selected trial chips as a complimentary addition to their coverage. Participants can explore new features while helping us evaluate and improve our products.

Trial devices may transmit performance and behavioral data to authorized Dreamer Works facilities for quality assurance, research, and product development.

"A brighter mind for a brighter tomorrow!"

Imagination Co.™

MANUFACTURING & PRODUCTION

The foundation of our animatronic family! Imagination Co. specializes in the design, assembly, and production of animatronic units for commercial attractions and approved business partners.

Our manufacturing teams combine mechanical engineering, character design, and advanced technology to create companions built to serve for years to come.

Imagination Co. works alongside Dreamer Company to support compatible AI systems and alongside Dreamer Insurance to help eligible owners protect their investments.
